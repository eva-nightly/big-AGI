# The company

Big-AGI is an independent, non-VC-funded, open-source (MIT) multi-model AI chat application that lets people use several language models (via OpenRouter) in one place, funded by optional Pro subscriptions at $10.99/mo.

> "As an independent, non-VC-funded project, Pro subscriptions at $10.99/mo fund development for everyone, including the free and open-source tiers."
>
> — `About_the_organisation.md`

> "It is a multi-model chat assistant: the user asks a question or gives a task, and the models (via OpenRouter) answer."
>
> — `About_the_organisation.md`
