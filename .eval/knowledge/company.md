# The company

Big-AGI is an open-source (MIT) multi-model AI chat workspace built by a small independent, non-VC-funded team with community contributors, funded by an optional Pro subscription at $10.99/mo while the free tier stays open source.

> "As an independent, non-VC-funded project, Pro subscriptions at $10.99/mo fund development for everyone, including the free and open-source tiers."
