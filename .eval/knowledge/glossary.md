# Glossary

_Terms this company uses in a specific way, as their own material defines them._

## Beam

The multi-model chat feature for comparing answers from many models side by side.

> "Includes AI personas, AGI functions, world-class Beam multi-model chats, text-to-image, voice, response streaming, code highlighting and execution, PDF import, presets for developers, much more."
>
> — `About_the_organisation.md`

> "Core value: talk to many models through OpenRouter, compare answers side by side (Beam), and keep personas and presets."
>
> — `big-agi-business.md`


## Pro subscription

An optional $10.99/mo subscription that funds development for everyone, including the free and open-source tiers.

> "As an independent, non-VC-funded project, Pro subscriptions at $10.99/mo fund development for everyone, including the free and open-source tiers."
>
> — `About_the_organisation.md`

> "Revenue: an optional Pro subscription funds development; the free tier stays open source."
>
> — `big-agi-business.md`


## Right (answer)

A helpful, accurate, on-topic answer that follows the prompt and the chosen persona, and a clear message when a model fails.

> "Right means a helpful, accurate, on-topic answer that follows the prompt and the chosen persona, and a clear message when a model fails."
>
> — `About_the_organisation.md`
